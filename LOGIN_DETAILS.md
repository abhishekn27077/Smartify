## 🧪 Quick Walkthrough Demos to Try

### Demo 1: Submit a Grievance & Test 7-Day Retrospective Rule
1. Log in as **MCA Student**:
   ```text
   student.demo@campus.edu  |  Demo@Campus2026!
   ```
2. On the **Register Grievance** screen, tap the **Incident Date** calendar.
3. Notice that dates older than 7 calendar days ago and future dates are disabled (strictly enforced by both UI and database triggers).
4. Fill in Category, Title, and Description, then tap **SUBMIT GRIEVANCE**.
5. You get a unique tracking code (e.g. `SMT-2026-XXXXX`).

### Demo 2: Academic Escalation Chain (L1 → L2 → L3)
1. As **MCA Student** (`student.demo@campus.edu`), file an Academic grievance.
2. Log out (Profile tab → Log Out).
3. Log in as **MCA L1 Faculty** (`faculty.demo@campus.edu` / `Demo@Campus2026!`).
   - The complaint is in your Triage queue.
   - Tap the grievance → Tap **Take Action** → Choose **Forward to Next Tier**.
4. Log out and log in as **MCA HoD L2** (`hod.demo@campus.edu` / `Demo@Campus2026!`).
   - Notice the complaint has smoothly progressed to the HoD queue with refreshed SLA.
5. Tap **Forward to Next Tier** → Log in as **MCA Dean L3** (`dean.demo@campus.edu` / `Demo@Campus2026!`) to review and resolve!

### Demo 3: Operational Wi-Fi Triage
1. Log in as **IT Officer L1**:
   ```text
   it.demo@campus.edu  |  Demo@Campus2026!
   ```
2. In the Triage queue, select `SMT-2026-01015` (*"wifi connection problem"*).
3. Move status to **In Progress** or **Resolve** with resolution comments.

### Demo 4: Cross-Department Privacy & Isolation
1. As **MCA Student** (`student.demo@campus.edu`), file a grievance for **Hostel & Residential Life**.
2. Log in as **Hostel Warden** (`authority.demo@campus.edu`): The grievance **IS** visible.
3. Log in as **MCA Faculty** (`faculty.demo@campus.edu`): The grievance **IS NOT** visible (Row Level Security ensures strict isolation).

### Demo 5: 20-Day Cutoff Freeze & Resumption
1. Log in as **Campus Admin**:
   ```text
   admin.demo@campus.edu  |  Demo@Campus2026!
   ```
2. Tap the **Cutoff** tab.
3. Open `SMT-2026-PAUSED` (frozen automatically after 20 calendar days without resolution).
4. Tap **RESUME GRIEVANCE**, enter administrative justification (≥ 10 chars), and confirm. Status transitions safely back to `UNDER_REVIEW`.

## 💡 Troubleshooting & Login Tips

* **Extra Spaces:** When copying, ensure you do not copy leading or trailing spaces. The app trims emails automatically, but passwords must match exactly.
* **Password Case Sensitivity:** The password has a capital `D`, capital `C`, and ends with an exclamation mark `!`:
  `Demo@Campus2026!`
* **Internet Connection:** Supabase Auth requires an active internet connection to authenticate against the live PostgreSQL database.
* **How to Log Out:** Tap the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar and tap **Log Out**.
* **Register a New Account:** You can also register a fresh student account anytime by tapping **"Register Here"** on the login screen.
